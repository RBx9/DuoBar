DuoBar 2.7.3 beta 4 is a test build for iOS 16, for Dopamine RootHide and standard Rootless (Dopamine, palera1n). It is not on Sileo. Stable 2.7.2 is unchanged.

## Fix

- **Control Center ring on Home-button iPhones:** on the iPhone 8, 8 Plus and SE, Control Center drew a larger DuoBar about 45 pt left of the Home Screen's, so the ring slid sideways and resized as Control Center opened and closed. It now matches the Home Screen's DuoBar in size and place. Measured in the iOS 16.4 simulator; Face ID iPhones were already identical and are unchanged.

## Not fixed

- The rotation-lock icon flicker in Control Center is still unexplained. The simulator could not show the icon.

## Install

Download the `.deb` for your jailbreak (`Rootless` for Dopamine Rootless or palera1n, `RootHide` for Dopamine RootHide), open it in your package manager and respring.
