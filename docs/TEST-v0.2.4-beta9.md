# DuoBar v0.2.4 Beta 9 — Rotation-lock spacing

**RootHide test build. Not published to Sileo.**

This is the confirmed-working Beta 8 code, with a single small adjustment: the target gap between the rotation-lock status icon and DuoBar in Control Center is increased from **5 to 10 points**, moving the lock icon about 5 points further left where space permits.

The Personal Hotspot chain-link inside DuoBar, the no-flash transitions, the 70–140% size slider, and the native-icon restoration logic are unchanged.

Install `DuoBar_0.2.4-beta9_RootHide.deb` on iOS 16 Dopamine RootHide and respring. Test Control Center with rotation lock enabled, repeatedly open and close it to check that stock icons do not flash, and connect to a hotspot to verify the chain link stays inside the circle. Turn DuoBar off to verify the stock layout returns.

**Rollback:** [Beta 8](https://github.com/RBx9/DuoBar/releases/tag/v0.2.4-beta.8) · [Stable v0.2.3](https://github.com/RBx9/DuoBar/releases/tag/v0.2.3).
